# Privacy

Typeset collects no data. There is no account, analytics, or tracking.

The app has no network access. It reads files and folders you choose to open, and saves source edits back to the file you chose. Recent files, folder access, and reading preferences stay on your Mac.

For the current policy, visit [typeset.md/privacy](https://typeset.md/privacy). Questions: [support@typeset.md](mailto:support@typeset.md).
